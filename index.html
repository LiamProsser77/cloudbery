What is Cloudbery?

Cloudbery is a social platform designed to bring people together. Instead of just scrolling through posts, you can discover people with similar interests, watch short videos, share content, and participate in communities.

What Can You Do?

Connect With People

Find people who enjoy the same hobbies, interests, and topics as you. Make connections and discover new communities.

Watch Short Videos

Discover short videos, share your own content, and explore what other people are creating.

Join Communities

Find communities built around the things you enjoy. Share ideas, participate in discussions, and meet new people.

Open Source Social Media

Cloudbery is built around the idea that social media should be more open. An open-source platform gives people the opportunity to inspect, contribute to, and improve the software.

Something New Is Growing.
